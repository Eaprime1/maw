# charge_tank_mechanics

- Sacred Pools accumulate **Resonance Charge** at stable one-hertz pulse.
- Mission completion, Ka-pressure milestones, and pattern recognition accelerate charge fill.

## Trigger (Flush)

When pool resonance reaches critical balance, a flush triggers: a sudden pressure release driven by Gravitar pull, moving the full pool over the edge with momentum.

## Stream Integrity (Testa Membrane)

Immediately before flush, clustered documents are wrapped in a **Testa Membrane** (synergy-shard surface tension). This keeps transcript, references, and core files bound as one stream body through Maw compression.
